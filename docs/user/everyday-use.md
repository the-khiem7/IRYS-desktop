## The routine

IRYS counts down in the background. When a break is due, it asks you to look into the distance while a short rest timer runs. You can keep the settings window closed; the tray tooltip shows when the next break is due.

## During a reminder

- Follow the rest prompt and let the countdown finish.
- Choose **Snooze** to postpone the reminder briefly.
- Choose **Skip** when you cannot take this break.
- Press **Escape** to dismiss the reminder. The full-screen reminder never traps keyboard input.

## Pause and resume

Pause the routine from the settings window or tray when reminders would be disruptive. A paused routine stays visibly paused and does not count toward the next break until you resume it.

On Windows, IRYS can reset a due break after you have already been away and can defer reminders while another app is full-screen. These behaviours are controlled in [Settings](/IRYS-desktop/user/settings/). On Linux, away and full-screen detection are currently safe no-ops.

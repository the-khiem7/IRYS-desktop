IRYS is a tray-first desktop reminder for the 30-30-30 routine: every 30 minutes, look roughly 30 feet (10 metres) away for 30 seconds.

## Choose your path

- **Using IRYS?** Start with [installation and first run](/IRYS-desktop/user/install-first-run/), then learn the short [everyday routine](/IRYS-desktop/user/everyday-use/).
- **Contributing?** Begin with the [project scope](/IRYS-desktop/contributors/overview/) and [architecture](/IRYS-desktop/contributors/architecture/).
- **Maintaining the build environment?** Use the [Docker and WSL runbook](/IRYS-desktop/runbooks/docker-build-environment/).

IRYS is a reminder app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat eye conditions.
